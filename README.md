## Teaching and research

I teach biochemistry, genetics, molecular biology, biotechnology, and biostatistics at
Roosevelt University. My work includes open educational resources, education-focused software,
and computational tools for biology and bioinformatics.

My current affiliation is the Department of Biological and Physical Sciences.

My structural biology research includes computational analysis of viruses and ribosomes,
methods for three-dimensional electron microscopy, protein geometry, and structure visualization.
I earned my Ph.D. in Molecular Biophysics and Biochemistry at Yale University and bachelor's
degrees in mathematics and physics at Iowa State University.

- Open educational resources: <https://biologyproblems.org/>
- Research and teaching software: <https://github.com/vosslab>
- Contact: <mailto:nvoss@roosevelt.edu>
